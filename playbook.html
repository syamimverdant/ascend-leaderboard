💰 XP — How You Earn Points

XP (Experience Points) is the currency that drives your progression. Every action counts.

ActivityXP
1 NOC Closed100 XP
Referral NOC+50 XP
MQL NOC+40 XP
Tier 2 Achievement+150 XP
Tier 3 Achievement+250 XP
Tier 4 Achievement+500 XP

Quarterly Team Target Achieved+400 XP
Quarter Complete (hit target all 3 months)+500 XP
Referral Champion+300 XP
MQL Champion+300 XP
Monthly NOC Champion+500 XP
Overachiever (each NOC above 12)+100 XP (NO CAP)

XP Formula

Total XP = (NOC × 100) + (Referral × 50) + (MQL × 40) + Tier Bonus + Champion Bonus + Overachiever Bonus + Team Bonus + Quarter Complete Bonus

Key Rules

  • XP never resets — it accumulates all year for Level progression.
  • The leaderboard resets at H1/H2 for fair competition.
  • XP has no monetary value — it tracks your growth and unlocks recognition.
  • There are multiple paths to earn XP — sales, referrals, MQLs, consistency, teamwork.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does XP expire?
No. XP is cumulative throughout FY27.

Q: Can Levels decrease?
No. Once achieved, Levels are permanent.

Q: Does the leaderboard reset?
Yes — at the start of H1 (July) and H2 (January). Your Level and XP stay, but rankings start fresh.

Q: Are cash rewards linked to XP?
No. They are separate systems that complement each other.

Q: I joined mid-year. How does it work?
You start fresh as Explorer with 0 XP. Compete on equal footing when the next Half resets.

Q: Is there a cap on Overachiever XP?
No! Every NOC above 12 earns +100 XP with no limit. Sky's the limit. 🚀

Q: What is Quarter Complete XP?
Hit your monthly target in all 3 months of a quarter = +500 XP bonus.
